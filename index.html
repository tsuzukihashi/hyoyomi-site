App Store で見る（準備中）

こんな困りごとのために作りました

答えや解説が間違っている

答え・選択肢・解説は、どれも図表の数字から1つの式で計算して出しています。全問を別の計算で解き直す検査と、答えを伏せて別の目で解き直す確かめを通しました。それでも誤りを見つけたら、解説の下の「この問題の誤りを知らせる」から送れます。

同じ問題ばかりで、すぐ覚えてしまう

図表は 607 セット、問題は全部で 3,867 問。同じ数字の表は1つもありません。

時間の感覚がつかめない

解いている間、本番の残り時間の帯と、この問題にかかっている秒数が出ます。結果では1問ずつの秒数と、本番の目安を並べます。

アプリの電卓が使いにくい

電卓は1文字ずつ消せて（⌫）、計算の履歴が残り、直前の答えをそのまま式に入れられます。出し入れしても中身は消えません。

ひょうよみの流れ

まず3問で、いまのペースを測る

受けるテスト（玉手箱・C-GAB／Web-GAB／まだ分からない）を選ぶと、図表の問題を3問解いて、あなたの1問あたりの秒数と本番の目安を並べます。

毎日「今日のセット」

ホームのボタン1つで、時間を計って10問。答えるとすぐ正誤と解説が出て、計算に使う数字が図表の上で光ります。

まちがえた問題を解き直す

まちがえた問題と、時間切れで答えられなかった問題は「まちがい」に並びます。正解すると外れます。

締切の前は、本番の長さで通す

締切を入れると、あと何日かが出ます。2日前からは、本番と同じ問題数と時間の通し練習をすすめます。

できること

図表の読み取り1,847問。表・棒グラフ・折れ線グラフ・帯グラフ、2つの表を組み合わせるもの。割合・増加率・指数・1人あたり・構成比など
四則逆算1,500問。□に入る数を、式を逆にほどいて求めます。分数と小数が混ざる式、右辺にも式がある形も

本番の長さ受けるテストの一般的な目安の問題数と時間で通して解けます。時間が来たら終わり、解説は最後にまとめて出ます
記録1問あたりの秒数と正答率の移り変わりを、形式ごとにグラフで

For iPhone · Numerical section of Japanese web aptitude tests

Pick numbers from charts.
Calculate before time runs out.

Hyoyomi helps you practice the numerical section of the web aptitude tests used in Japanese job hunting (Tamatebako, GAB, C-GAB): chart reading, reverse arithmetic, and table blanks, at the real test's pace. For every question, you see your time next to the real test's target.

View on the App Store (coming soon)

A paid app for iPhone. One purchase, nothing more to pay, and no ads. The questions are in Japanese, since they prepare you for tests taken in Japanese.

Built for these problems

Wrong answers or explanations

Every answer, choice, and explanation is calculated from the chart's numbers with a single formula. All questions pass a check that re-solves them with separate code, plus blind re-solving by another reviewer. If you still find a mistake, send it from “Report a Problem with This Question” under the explanation.

Too few questions to stay fresh

607 chart sets and 3,867 questions in all. No two tables have the same numbers.

No feel for the time limit

While you solve, a bar shows the remaining time and how long you've spent on the current question. Results list your time for each question next to the test's target.

Clumsy built-in calculators

Delete one character at a time (⌫), keep a history of calculations, and insert the last answer into your next calculation. Its contents stay when you hide it.

How it works

Measure your pace with 3 questions

Choose your test (Tamatebako / C-GAB, Web-GAB, or Not Sure Yet), solve 3 chart questions, and see your seconds per question next to the test's target.

Today's Set, every day

One button on Home starts 10 timed questions. Each answer shows right or wrong with an explanation, and the numbers you need light up on the chart.

Retry your mistakes

Questions you missed or ran out of time on are listed in Mistakes. Answer one correctly to remove it.

Before the deadline, go full length

Enter your deadline to see the days left. From 2 days before, a full-length run with the test's usual number of questions and time is suggested.

FAQ

Are these real test questions?

No. This is an independent practice app made by an individual and is not affiliated with the test provider. Questions are generated by calculation from chart data and are not copies of real tests or published workbooks.

Is it available in English?

The app's screens are in English, but the questions and charts are in Japanese, because they prepare you for tests taken in Japanese.

I found a mistake in a question

Tap “Report a Problem with This Question” under the explanation to send an email with the question number. It will be checked and fixed in the next version.

What happens to my records when I change phones?

A backup of your records is kept in your iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on your new iPhone, and you can pick up where you left off.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow Apple's policy. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer can't issue refunds directly.
